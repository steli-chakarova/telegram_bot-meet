Privacy Policy — Steli Meet Bot

Last updated: 2026-10-09

This bot creates Google Meet links when you use the /meet command in Telegram.

What data is used

  • Your Telegram user id / chat id (to allow or deny access to /meet)
  • Google OAuth tokens stored on the bot server, used only to create Meet spaces
  • Optional custom message texts stored for /msg replies

What we do not do

  • We do not sell data
  • We do not share Meet contents or chat messages with third parties
  • Google Meet sessions themselves are governed by Google’s policies
